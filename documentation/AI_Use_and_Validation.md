# AI Use and Validation Record

## Intended use

AI assistance is appropriate for drafting formula patterns, outlining a dashboard and improving the first draft of a written summary. It is not a source of candidate facts or a substitute for checking calculations.

## Example prompt used in this portfolio workflow

“Given only this clearly synthetic recruitment funnel table and its stage counts, write a concise leadership summary. Separate observed counts from possible explanations. Do not infer causes, do not recommend source budget changes from small groups, and explicitly state that the data is synthetic.”

## Checks performed by the analyst

- Reconciled raw records, duplicate removals and final unique candidate count.
- Recomputed funnel counts from Current Stage ordering.
- Compared source and role summaries with underlying candidate rows.
- Checked the definition and denominator for each percentage.
- Kept causal explanations as follow-up questions rather than claims.
- Removed direct identifiers and included a synthetic-data notice.

## Safe handling rule

Never paste real candidate or employee personal data, interview notes, compensation details or other confidential records into an AI service unless the employer has explicitly approved that service and its data handling. Use a minimal, de-identified sample and verify every output.
