# Presentation Speaker Notes

## Slide 1: Recruitment Funnel Analytics

“I built this portfolio case around a fictional quantum computing and AI company. The data covers 360 applications submitted from January through June 2026, observed at the end of September. Everything in the project is synthetic.”

## Slide 2: Most applications leave before the interview loop

“This view counts candidates at the furthest stage they reached. Forty-eight candidates joined from 360 applications, giving a 13.3% application-to-join rate. The median time from application to start date was 33 days for those who joined.”

## Slide 3: The biggest count loss occurs at the first interview handoff

“The largest absolute gap is 100 candidates between screening and recruiter interview. Another 80 do not appear at the hiring manager interview stage. The data does not tell us why. I would check how quickly recruiters contact candidates, how screen criteria vary by role, and whether exit reasons are captured.”

## Slide 4: Channel results point to a question

“The Careers Page rate is 16.9%, while the agency rate is 4.8%. There are only 77 and 42 applications in those groups, and the sample does not control for role mix. I would compare similar roles and experience levels and include channel cost before recommending a change.”

## Slide 5: A short follow-up can turn this snapshot into an operating view

“My recommendations are to review the early handoffs, capture structured disposition reasons, compare channel results within comparable roles and experience bands, and separate accepted, declined and joined offer statuses. The sample has six duplicate IDs, five missing sources and two invalid milestone dates, all documented in the project.”
